# Cdawg Character Bible

Cdawg is the Cdawg Arcade mascot: a stylized cartoon dog built for readable 2D game animation, Discord Activities, stickers, and future brand art.

## Core Identity

- Stylized cartoon dog arcade mascot.
- Dark charcoal to black fur.
- Cream muzzle and chest.
- Orange hoodie or varsity-style jacket.
- Orange `C` emblem on the chest.
- White sneakers.
- Expressive eyebrows.
- Semi-floppy ears.
- Confident but slightly dopey personality.
- Exaggerated physical comedy.
- Clean 2D vector-friendly shapes.

Cdawg is not realistic and should not be rendered in a furry-anime style.

## Silhouette

Cdawg should read clearly at small Discord Activity sizes:

- Large rounded head.
- Semi-floppy ears that break the head silhouette.
- Compact torso.
- Short, chunky limbs.
- Visible tail for balance and comedy.
- Oversized sneakers for readable foot placement.
- Jacket shape broad enough to carry the `C` emblem.

## Proportions

- Head: about 40% of full standing height.
- Torso: short and stocky, about 30% of full standing height.
- Arms: long enough to brace outward during wobble poses.
- Legs: shorter than arms, ending in oversized shoes.
- Muzzle: large cream oval that keeps expressions readable.
- Eyebrows: high-contrast cream or orange marks that carry most emotion.

## Palette

- Fur: `#151515` dark charcoal.
- Fur highlight: `#252323`.
- Muzzle and chest: `#FFEFD0`.
- Jacket orange: `#FF8A22`.
- Jacket highlight: `#FFC266`.
- Sneakers: `#F7F2E8`.
- Ink/outline: `#08080C`.

The palette should stay high-contrast against the dark arcade stage.

## Facial Expressions

- Idle: relaxed eyebrows, small straight mouth.
- Leaning: one brow raised, mouth slightly open.
- Bracing: eyebrows angled inward, clenched mouth.
- Wobble: uneven eyes and stretched mouth.
- Panic: wide mouth, high brows, ears lifted or flared.
- Impact: squashed muzzle, flattened body, comic shock.
- Victory: confident grin, lifted arms, bright eyes.

## Clothing

Cdawg wears an orange hoodie or varsity-style jacket. The jacket should feel sporty, arcade-branded, and simple enough for vector animation. The chest must include a readable orange or dark `C` emblem depending on jacket contrast.

## Personality

Cdawg is brave, cocky, and a little unaware of danger. He thinks he has the run under control until the platform starts moving. The comedy should come from overconfidence, delayed panic, and exaggerated recovery attempts.

## Animation Language

- Snappy squash and stretch.
- Big readable lean poses.
- Arms flail only when pressure is high.
- Tail acts as a counterweight.
- Shoes slide and stomp during correction.
- Impact uses a comic hit pause, camera shake, and short burst particles.
- New records should feel triumphant, not sarcastic.

## Forbidden Directions

- Realistic dog anatomy.
- Furry-anime styling.
- Horror, gore, or aggressive animal behavior.
- Overly detailed fur rendering.
- Photorealism.
- Tiny unreadable facial features.
- Brand marks other than the Cdawg `C`.
- Costumes that obscure the orange jacket identity.

## Balance-Specific Poses

- Idle: centered on the platform, knees soft, tail low.
- Lean left: torso and head shift left, right arm counterbalances.
- Lean right: mirrored lean pose.
- Brace: feet planted wide, arms out, brows focused.
- Wobble: body offset from feet, tail swings opposite tilt.
- Panic: mouth open, ears lifted, arms flailing.
- Fall: body rotates with platform direction, shoes trailing.
- Impact: squashed body, `WHAM!` energy burst, shoes kicked out.
- New-record victory: arms raised, chest emblem forward, confident grin.
