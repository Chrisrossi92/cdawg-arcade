---
# Welcome to CDAWG Arcade 🐾
A little dog. A lot of attitude.

This is our home for playing, sharing scores, and hanging out. We’re starting small with **CDAWG Balance**: keep Cdawg upright for as long as you can.

**Jump in**
1. Open Discord’s **Apps launcher** in this channel, choose **CDAWG Arcade**, and select **Launch**.
2. In the lobby, choose **Balance**, then **Start Game**.
3. Hold **← / →**, **A / D**, or the on-screen **Left / Right** buttons to lean. Let go to ease off.

Official scores saved in Discord appear in your personal results and this server’s leaderboard. Practice scores stay on your device. Keep the game in focus during a run; switching away or an interruption makes that run practice only.

Choose **Play Again** for another go, or **Back to Arcade** to return to the lobby. Open your player menu for sound settings and help; **Less motion** is available in the lobby settings.

Share your scores here. Ideas or something not working? Use the **Feedback & Bugs** thread attached to this post.

One game to start. Plenty of room for a good rivalry.
---

Got an idea, a rough edge, or a bug? Drop it here. Feedback on the lobby, controls, artwork, and clarity is welcome.

For a bug, tell us:
- What you were trying to do.
- What happened, and what you expected instead.
- Whether you’re using desktop Discord, a browser, or mobile.
- Roughly when it happened, and whether it happened again.
